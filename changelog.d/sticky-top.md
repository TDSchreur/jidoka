## Changed

- The toolbar, the andon strip and On the line stay at the top of the dashboard while you scroll the Shipped list; on a narrow screen the toolbar scrolls away and the strip and the line stay. They take at most 60% of the window: with many active Runs the line's rows scroll on their own, and so does a long list of waiting Runs in the strip. In a very short window, such as at 400% zoom, they scroll with the page.

## Fixed

- On a phone, the Shipped table no longer squeezes run titles to a few letters per line. It keeps readable columns and scrolls sideways inside its own area.
